Blockbound

Mine · build · walk the world around

  1. Move with WASD / arrows or the left stick. Space / jump button to jump. Hold Shift to sprint.
  2. Tap to place the selected block. Hold on a block to dig it up.
  3. Open Craft (C / E). Place a Workbench for better recipes. Furnace smelts ores.
  4. Worlds loop left/right. Dig to magma; the sky is the ceiling.
  5. Singleplayer opens your world list — name worlds, pick a seed, difficulty, and size.
  6. Same seed + size regenerates the same terrain. Share seeds with friends.
  7. Rotate your device for portrait or landscape play.
  8. Difficulty: Creative / Easy / Normal / Hard. Creative: ✦ Blocks (G) for every item.
