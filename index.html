Blockbound

Mine · build · walk the world around

  1. iPad / Kids mode (on by default for touch): tap where to walk, tap blocks to dig, pick a block then tap to build. Big colored button appears to Open / Eat / Hit — no keyboard ever needed. Drag to look around. Pinch with two fingers to zoom in/out.
  2. Classic (Options): stick + JUMP. Hold to dig, tap to place. Same big action button on tablets.
  3. Open Craft (⚒ / C). Pick a recipe, then press CRAFT. Place a Workbench for tools. Furnace smelts ores.
  4. In caves, place ladders on any open air (the back wall counts). Stack ladders to climb.
  5. Walk at a one-block step and you hop up on your own — no jump needed. Turn Auto-jump off in Options if you'd rather jump yourself.
  6. In water you swim: hold JUMP / up to rise, and let go to sink.
  7. Deep down, a hard Magma crust caps the molten Lava. Mine the crust and you fall in — you'll sink and burn, so swim straight back out. Pour water on lava to freeze it into stone, mine that, and work your way down to bedrock.
  8. Worlds loop left/right; the sky is the ceiling.
  9. Singleplayer opens your world list — name worlds, pick a seed, difficulty, and size.
  10. Same seed + size regenerates the same terrain. Share seeds with friends.
  11. Rotate your device for portrait or landscape play.
  12. Options turns the coordinates, the minimap and auto-jump on or off, and switches between Kids and Classic controls.
  13. Difficulty: Creative / Easy / Normal / Hard. Creative: ✦ Blocks (G) for every item, plus ✈ Fly (JUMP=up, DOWN=down, double-tap JUMP or ✈ to toggle).
