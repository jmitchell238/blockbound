  1. Move with WASD / arrows or the left stick. Space / jump button to jump.
  2. Tap to place the selected block. Hold on a block to dig it up.
  3. Open Craft (C / E) — turn wood into planks, sticks, torches, tools. Place a Workbench for better recipes.
  4. The world loops left/right — pick a size up to 16,384 blocks (Blockheads-scale). Dig down to magma; the sky is the ceiling.
  5. 🎒 Inventory — backpack holds 24 stacks. Craft a Chest, place it, F to store more.
  6. Workbench unlocks tools & swords. Furnace smelts ores. Craft menu has All / Basic / Tools / Smelt.
  7. ⚔ Hit punches or sword-swipes. Night: zombies & skeletons. Day/night: wolves. Rabbits hop around (food).
  8. 1–8 hotbar · F use · Esc pause · scroll zoom.
